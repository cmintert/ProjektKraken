Write 50-100 words. Focus on the most distinctive features. Sharp, vivid, minimal — every word counts.

GUIDELINES:
- 50-100 words maximum
- One or two core details that define the element
- Sensory language that creates immediate atmosphere
- No exposition dumps or extended backstory
- Leave room for the reader's imagination
